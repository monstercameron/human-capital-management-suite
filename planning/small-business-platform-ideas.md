# Small-business platform: adopted expansion and refinement plan

Status: **ADOPTED FOR PLANNING AND IMPLEMENTATION BACKLOG — PHASE_3 / PHASE_4.**
Started: 2026-10-22. Last refined: 2026-10-22. The owner explicitly authorized detailed plans, implementation issues, subsystem reuse, and operational gap backfill.

## Product direction

> One workspace for running a small business, built around its people and the work they do.

The HR and workflow foundation remains the entry point. Optional business modules extend it into connected operations. Professional services, trades, recurring maintenance, repair and property services are reference scenarios, not validated market commitments. A business may begin with one owner and enable only the operations it needs.

The master [plan](plan.md#313-small-business-operations-expansion-2026-10-22), [execution plan](execution-plan.md), domain specifications and [TODO contracts](todos.md) now authorize this later-phase expansion. Planning approval does not establish implemented functionality, vendor qualification, staffed ownership, or production release. Existing Phase 1 gates and unfinished work retain their meaning.

The complete implementation specifications, UI/UX contracts, acceptance criteria and per-ID review histories are stored in [the expansion contract registry](../definitions/planning/smb-expansion.json). That file is planning evidence, not a second runtime schema or capability registry. The Markdown TODOs remain the authoritative atomic work list; their generated summary is `definitions/planning/todo-registry.json`.

## Shared business needs and module boundaries

People and access, customer/supplier relationships, requests and commitments, scheduled work, documents, conversations, supplies/equipment, commercial records, visibility and connectivity recur across these segments. A workflow coordinates the handoffs; each existing domain owns its business facts, corrections and authority.

The twelve business subsystems are relationships, jobs, scheduling, quotes/orders, invoices/payments, inventory, equipment, purchasing, external portals, accounting connectivity, device connectivity and setup. Shared CORE work composes existing platform services; it is not a thirteenth business application.

Reuse the actual source owner before adding code. Recruiting CRM keeps recruiting semantics; employment service-credit keeps workforce semantics; `internal/commercial` keeps suite subscription/entitlement semantics. Business parties do not become employees. Customer receivables and supplier payables receive explicit domain-owned extensions, not the suite billing ledger.

## Experience contract

Use the existing Go SSR/WASM, GoWebComponents, productui, page/floorplan/widget and policy-aware projection foundations. Keep Home, My Work, People, Organization, Insights and Admin as the global shell. Business hubs, search, contextual links and user pins expose enabled work without twelve new permanent navigation destinations.

Each issue defines actors, proposed route placement, fields, actions, validation, empty/loading/denied/error/conflict/offline/completed states and recovery. Required UI qualification covers desktop 1280×720, 390px and 320px widths; light/dark; en-US, de-DE and Arabic RTL; keyboard navigation, meaningful labels and focus, reduced motion, long text and browser Back/reload. API-only contracts identify their consuming flow and truthful error projection.

External portals reuse the rendering and capability stack with independent grants, allowed fields and object scope. Navigation visibility, a matching email, a contact affiliation or a device pairing never confers business authority. Domains own posted operations; offline capture and local queues show pending/unknown until durable server reconciliation.

## Delivery sequence and release proof

1. Admit optional modules, real intent definitions/capabilities, current authorization and API adoption through existing registries. Definition names are not executable authority until their source, closed vocabularies, publication lifecycle and proofs are added.
2. Establish business parties and existing work-order extensions. Add service-only quote/job/invoice flows without requiring stock, device, payroll or full accounting modules to be enabled.
3. Connect scheduling, stock, procurement and equipment as needed, then the commercial corrections and accounting reconciliation that depend on them. Implementation dependency ordering is distinct from per-company module activation.
4. Add scoped portals, supported devices and setup/import experiences through their existing owners. Qualify every supported device/protocol/platform combination; do not promise universal USB/LAN access.
5. Demonstrate the connected reference loops below, their error/recovery cases and authorized reporting before enabling production use.

- Request → quote → acceptance → scheduled job → approved time/materials/expenses → completion → invoice → receipt → accounting reconciliation; include amendment, cancellation, complaint/rework and credit/refund.
- Shortage → reviewed purchase → supplier dispatch → partial receipt/inspection → usable stock/equipment → supplier bill match → payable/payment; include rejected goods, supplier return, replacement/credit and reconciliation.
- Spreadsheet import → dry run → approved commands → corrected opening facts → launch → recover/export/exit; retain evidence and source authority across interruptions.

All new TODOs remain unchecked. A successful library test, generated client, route registration or planning approval is not served end-to-end proof. Domain state and outbox commit atomically where owned together; cross-domain handoffs use explicit idempotent contracts, durable receipts and reconciliation rather than an assumed distributed transaction.
